 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using **Power BI** to identify churn patterns, revenue concentration, customer risk, contract behavior, and factors associated with customer attrition.

The goal of this project is to transform customer data into meaningful business insights that can help organizations improve **customer retention and reduce churn**.


 Business Objectives

* Analyze the overall customer churn rate
* Identify customer groups with higher churn risk
* Analyze monthly charges across customer types
* Understand the relationship between tenure and churn
* Analyze churn by contract type
* Identify patterns related to OnlineSecurity and OnlineBackup
* Compare monthly charges between retained and churned customers
* Provide actionable recommendations for improving retention

 Key Insights

 1. Revenue Concentration

Existing customers contribute the majority of monthly charges:

* Existing Customers: **375.03K**
* New Customers: **81.08K**

This shows that existing customers represent a significant portion of monthly revenue.

 2. Early Churn Risk

Customers in their first **0–6 months** have the highest churn rate at **52.94%**.

This indicates that early customer onboarding and engagement are important areas for retention.

 3. Contract Type

Month-to-month customers form the largest churned customer group:

* Month-to-Month: **1,655**
* One Year: **166**
* Two Year: **48**

Longer-term contracts are associated with lower attrition in this analysis.

 4. Monthly Charges

Average monthly charges are higher among churned customers:

* Retained: **$61**
* Churned: **$74**

This suggests that customers with higher monthly charges represent an important segment for retention analysis.

 5. Customer Risk

Among retained customers:

* Normal Risk: **4,541**
* High Risk: **633**

The high-risk segment can be monitored for potential early intervention.

 6. Services

Monthly charges were also analyzed based on OnlineSecurity and OnlineBackup usage.

OnlineSecurity:

* No: **157.44K**
* Yes: **94.43K**
* No Internet Service: **32.24K**

OnlineBackup:

* No: **107.28K**
* Yes: **64.72K**

---
 Business Recommendations

 Targeted Retention

Focus retention activities on customers in the **0–6 month tenure group** and customers with higher monthly charges.

 Contract Incentives

Encourage month-to-month customers to move toward longer-term plans through suitable contract incentives.

 Service Bundles

Promote OnlineSecurity and OnlineBackup bundles to increase customer value and engagement.

 Project Outcome

The analysis identified several important churn patterns, particularly **high churn during the first six months**, a large concentration of churn among **month-to-month customers**, and higher average monthly charges among churned customers.

These insights can help businesses develop targeted retention strategies and prioritize high-risk customer segments.
